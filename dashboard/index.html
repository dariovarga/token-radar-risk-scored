How the score works

Raw implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −50…+100%, weighted 0.6. Maturity is 0.5 + min(age, 1460 days) / 1460 × 0.5; unknown age counts as 365 days.

Risk score (0–100) = concentration + momentum + age + category + scale, each 0–20. One chain scores 20, six or more score 2. A violent 7-day swing scores up to 20; no reported change scores 12. Very young (<90 days) scores 18 and very old without a token (>5 years) scores 17 — a launch that may never come — while 1–3 years scores 4. Category scores 5 for Dexs and Lending up to 15 for wrappers and exotic structures. TVL under $10M scores 18; over $1B scores 2.

Adjusted value = raw × (1 − 0.5 × risk / 100). Rank 1 is the highest adjusted value. Grades: A 0–30 Low, B 31–42 Guarded, C 43–52 Moderate, D 53–62 Elevated, E 63–72 High, F 73–100 Severe. Every farm plan sizes its position to the grade — a severe-risk farm is a small farm by construction.

A protocol “records no token” when DefiLlama records no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol for it. That is the record, not proof: a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued.
